How the number is made

A protocol's implied value is its TVL times a launch multiple learned from the snapshot's own live-token protocols, times a growth multiplier from its seven-day change, times a maturity multiplier from how long it has run without a token. Nothing is hand-listed: no protocol name is special-cased, and every row can be recomputed from the figures on it.

  1. Launch multiple: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols that already trade, taken per category and shrunk toward the whole market when a category is thin.
  2. Growth multiplier: seven-day TVL change, clamped to a sane band, so one noisy week cannot decide the board.
  3. Maturity multiplier: a longer run without a token raises the value, up to three years; an unknown listing date is treated as one year.
